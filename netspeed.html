Privacy Policy

NetSpeed 5G: Gamer Ping Radar

Effective Date: October 4, 2026 | Last Updated: October 4, 2026

Privacy Summary: NetSpeed 5G: Gamer Ping Radar is built with a strict Privacy-First architecture. We do NOT collect, sell, monetize, or track your personal information, contact lists, browsing history, or precise GPS coordinates. All network test histories are stored exclusively on your device.

1. Information We Collect and Process

NetSpeed 5G is a network diagnostics tool. To measure bandwidth and server latency accurately, the app processes the following technical network data strictly during active user sessions:

  • Network Performance Metrics: Download speeds, upload speeds, ICMP/TCP ping latency, jitter, and packet loss percentages.
  • Connection Type & Status: Whether you are connected via Wi-Fi, 5G, 4G LTE, or Ethernet (used solely to display connection type in your test history).
  • Public IP & ISP Identification: During active tests, your public IP and ISP carrier name may be retrieved from standard network lookup endpoints to display your ISP on the test card. This information is not stored on any remote database operated by us.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

We respect your digital privacy. The application explicitly does NOT:

  • Require or create user accounts, logins, or social profile links.
  • Access your contacts, calendar, SMS, camera, photos, or microphone.
  • Track background GPS location.
  • Monitor your web browsing history, DNS queries outside the app, or app usage outside of NetSpeed 5G.

3. Local Data Storage & User Control

Your speed test logs (timestamp, speed results, ping, and network type) are saved locally on your device in secure local storage:

  • This data never leaves your device and is never sent to our servers.
  • You can clear your entire test history at any time directly in the app under Settings → Data & Privacy → Clear Saved Test History.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all locally saved history.

4. Third-Party Services & Network Testing Endpoints

To measure download/upload speeds and ping latency to gaming ecosystems, the app transmits diagnostic packets to public cloud nodes and test servers:

  • Speed Test CDNs: High-bandwidth speed test nodes (e.g., Cloudflare, public CDN mirrors) to measure download and upload throughput.
  • Game Server Targets: Public ICMP/HTTP ping endpoints operated by gaming providers (e.g., Riot Games, Valve/Steam, Epic Games, AWS, Google Cloud) solely to measure round-trip latency.
  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution, updates, and licensing. Refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

NetSpeed 5G is a general-audience network utility tool. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). The app does not collect personal data from any user regardless of age.

6. Security of Your Data

Because NetSpeed 5G does not maintain remote user databases or collect personal user accounts, there is no centralized database vulnerable to data breaches or credential leaks.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain compliance with Google Play Store policies and legal regulations. Updates will be published on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
